outdoor_grill The Craft & The Hearth

Born in The Heights, Rooted in Smoke.

In 2018, Pitmaster Marcus Vance rolled a custom 500-gallon steel offset smoker into an empty lot along West 19th Street. His ethos was simple: honor the patient, pure methods of traditional Central Texas meat markets without compromise or corner-cutting.

Every cut begins with ethically pasture-raised beef sourced directly from Texas ranches. We burn exclusively aged post oak cut from the Brazos River basin, balancing clean convective draft with overnight ember control. Zero electric thermostats. Zero liquid shortcuts. Just watchful eyes, touch, and intuition cultivated across thousands of fires.

When you step up to our carving counter, you taste the genuine result of an all-night watch in the Houston humidity—charred bark that shatters, melting brisket fat, and hospitality straight from our neighborhood heart.
